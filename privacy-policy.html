Privacy Policy — TI-84 Graphing Calculator

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

TI-84 Graphing Calculator is a graphing calculator app whose calculations run locally on your device. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Advertising and data processed by Google

The Android app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertising. Google's SDK may collect and share IP-based approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and app activity, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. This information is transmitted using encryption in transit. We do not operate an account system or our own analytics service.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices through the app's “Privacy & ad choices” menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Your calculator content

Expressions, graph functions, programs, matrices and lists are processed on your device and are not sent to AdMob by this app. These values are held in the current app session; exported programs are saved or shared only when you choose to do so.

Permissions and offline use

Internet and network-state permissions support advertising. The advertising ID permission supports AdMob. Core calculator functions work without internet; ads may not be available offline. We do not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions.

Children

This release is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Ad requests limit ad content to PG. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and consult Google's privacy controls for information processed by its services.

Retention and deletion

We do not maintain a server database of calculator content or user accounts. Closing the app clears session-only values. Google controls retention of information it processes; review its policy and account/device privacy controls for access and deletion choices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
