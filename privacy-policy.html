Privacy Policy — Graphing Calculator 84

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

Graphing Calculator 84 is a graphing calculator app that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history, functions, matrices, lists, and settings are kept only on your own device so the app can show them back to you. Nothing ever leaves the device.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files access).

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, there is nothing special to note for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
